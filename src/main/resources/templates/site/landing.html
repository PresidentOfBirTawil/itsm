Questions

Frequently asked

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The desktop app runs entirely on your computer, and a server installation only needs your own network. Webhooks to Slack or Teams need the internet, of course.

Is our incident text sent to an AI service?

Never. The classifier, the suggested fixes and the predictions are computed inside Quell from your own history. Nothing leaves your installation unless you connect a webhook.

How does Quell decide the priority?

Impact × urgency × how critical the service is, each from 1 to 3. The calculation is shown on every incident, and more people affected raises the impact automatically.

Can it work with the tools we already use?

Yes: webhooks post to Slack, Microsoft Teams or any app (signed, retried), and the REST API lets your monitoring report incidents and your scripts read them.

Can I try it without installing anything?

Yes. Open the live demo and sign in with one of the demo accounts on the sign-in page.
