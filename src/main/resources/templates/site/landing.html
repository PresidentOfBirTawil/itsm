FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Quell runs on your computer or your own server and works offline. Your data never leaves your organisation.

How does Quell decide the priority?

Impact × urgency × how critical the affected service is, each from 1 to 3. The score maps to P1 (critical) to P4 (low), and every incident shows the calculation, so nobody has to trust a black box.

What does "learns from every incident" mean?

Quell trains a classifier on your past incidents to suggest categories, ranks past fixes by similarity to suggest solutions, and uses real resolution times to predict which incidents will miss their deadline. Every suggestion shows why it was made.

Can I try it without installing anything?

Yes. Click "Try the demo" and sign in with one of the demo accounts: an employee, a technician or an administrator. Load realistic sample data from the administrator's settings.

Where is my data stored?

In a database file on your own computer or server. On the desktop app, the tray icon's "Show data folder" opens it.
